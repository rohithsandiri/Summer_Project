Gap Closure Checklist — SelfHealing_Gap_Analysis.docx applied to this project's draft

This maps every gap raised in the professor's reviewer-style gap analysis (written against your friend's simpler single-service report) to how the equivalent concern is handled in SelfHealing_BatchRollback_Paper_Draft.docx — our project's paper, not a copy of theirs. Our system and dataset differ, so some gaps are closed the same way; a few (statistical power) are closed by being honest about scope rather than by claiming rigor we don't have data for yet.

One structural difference worth flagging before submission

The reviewed report's central empirical claim was a human-vs-automation MTTR comparison. This project's paper does not attempt that comparison — it was never built to run one (there is no scripted human-baseline harness in this codebase). Instead, this draft's central claim is systems-oriented: dependency-aware batch rollback, two-signal verification applied uniformly across primary and peer rollbacks, and rollback-safety for the healer's own state — with MTTR reported only as illustrative, mechanism-level supporting evidence, not the headline result. This was a deliberate choice to avoid overclaiming with n=1–2 data rather than an oversight; you may want to discuss with B. Thangaraju whether the systems contribution alone is the right headline, or whether a human-baseline harness should be added before submission to make the paper more directly comparable to the reviewed genre.
